Tallow Studio

Tallow Studio is a small independent studio that makes short animated explainer videos for YouTube.

Our channels

  • Lost Machines: how old devices and forgotten materials really worked.
  • History's Footnotes: strange true events most people never heard of.
  • Failure Mode: engineering failures traced back to their first cause.

Tallow Studio Uploader

Tallow Studio Uploader is our own internal tool. It uploads our videos to our own YouTube channels, sets their titles, descriptions and privacy, and reads the channels' own statistics so we can see which videos work. It is used only by Tallow Studio for channels we own. It is not offered to the public and it does not access anyone else's account.
