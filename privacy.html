Privacy policy

Tallow Studio Uploader. Last updated 4 November 2026.

Who we are

Tallow Studio Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool run by Tallow Studio. It manages only the YouTube channels that Tallow Studio owns. Contact: tallowstudio.media@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to our own channels and set their title, description, thumbnail and privacy.
  • youtube.readonly: to read our own channel and video details, for example to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read aggregate statistics of our own channels, such as views and watch time.

The app does not access other people's channels, private messages, contacts or payment details. It does not collect personal information about viewers. Analytics data from YouTube is aggregate and does not identify individual viewers.

How data is used and stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on Tallow Studio's own computers and are used only to act on our own channels.
  • Aggregate channel statistics are stored on our own computers to plan future videos. They are kept for as long as the channel exists and deleted within 30 days after a channel is closed or the app is disconnected.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies

The app is a command-line tool and does not use cookies. This website does not use cookies or trackers.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. To ask us to delete stored data, email tallowstudio.media@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
